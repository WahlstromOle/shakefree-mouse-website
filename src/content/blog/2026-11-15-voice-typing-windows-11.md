To start voice typing in Windows 11, click in any text box, press the **Windows key and H**, wait until you see **Listening…**, and speak. Your words appear where the cursor is. To stop, say **"stop listening"** or select the microphone button.

## What you need

Microsoft lists three things:

- **An internet connection.** Voice typing uses Microsoft's online speech recognition.
- **A microphone.** The one built into most laptops and webcams works. A headset microphone close to your mouth usually picks you up more clearly.
- **The cursor in a text box.** Voice typing only starts when there's somewhere for the words to go, such as an email, a document or a search box.

## How to dictate, step by step

1. Click in the place you want to write. With a tremor, the click only needs to land somewhere in the box; you can move the cursor with the arrow keys afterwards.
2. Press the **Windows key and H**. A small voice typing bar appears.
3. Wait for **Listening…**, then speak normally, in full sentences.
4. Say **"stop listening"** when you're done, or select the microphone button on the bar.

If pressing two keys at once is hard, [Sticky Keys](/sticky-keys-windows-11) lets you press the **Windows key**, then **H**.

## Commands for punctuation and corrections

You can speak a few commands while voice typing is listening. In English these include:

- **"Delete that"** or **"Scratch that"** removes what you just said.
- **"Select that"** selects it, so you can retype or say it again.
- **"Press Enter"**, **"Press Backspace"**, **"Press Tab"** and **"Press Space"** press those keys for you.
- Say punctuation where you want it, for example **"comma"** or **"question mark"**.

Saying punctuation all the time is tiring. The next section shows how to let Windows add it for you.

## Settings worth changing

Select the **gear** on the voice typing bar to see its settings:

- **Automatic punctuation:** Windows adds full stops and commas from the way you speak. Most people find this much more relaxed.
- **Voice typing launcher:** shows a small microphone button when you're in a text box, so you can start voice typing with one click instead of the shortcut.
- **Default microphone:** choose which microphone to use if you have more than one.

You can also press the **Windows key, Alt and H** to move around the voice typing bar with the keyboard instead of the mouse.

## Languages

Voice typing works in many languages and follows the input language you're using. To switch, press the **Windows key and Spacebar**. To add a language, go to **Settings** > **Time & language** > **Language & region**.

## Voice typing or voice access?

Windows 11 has two voice features, and it's easy to mix them up:

- **Voice typing** (**Windows key and H**) is for writing. It types what you say into a text box.
- **Voice access** controls the whole PC by voice: opening apps, clicking buttons, scrolling, and dictating too. Microsoft notes that voice access can be started at any time, while voice typing only starts when the cursor is in a text box.

If writing is your main difficulty, start with voice typing. If clicking and moving the mouse are hard too, voice access may suit you better. Our [guide to Windows 11 accessibility settings for hand tremors](/windows-11-accessibility-tremor) explains how to set up voice access. If voice access is already running and you press the **Windows key and H**, Windows tells you that you can use voice access for typing too.

## Tips for better results

- **Speak in whole sentences** rather than word by word. It gives the speech recognition more context.
- **Keep background noise down** where you can: radio, TV and open windows.
- **Check before you send.** Voice typing can mishear names and unusual words. Read the text back, and fix small errors with the keyboard.
- **Mix voice and keys.** Many people dictate the bulk of a message and use the keyboard for small fixes. Our tips for [typing with a hand tremor](/typing-with-hand-tremor) cover the keyboard side.

AbilityNet, a UK charity, suggests voice recognition for people with tremors whose voice is clear. It takes a little patience to get used to, but it can replace a lot of typing.

## Privacy

Standard voice typing sends what you say to Microsoft's online speech service to turn it into text. If that matters to you, check Microsoft's privacy information before using it for sensitive material. On some newer Copilot+ PCs, a feature called fluid dictation does part of the processing on the PC itself.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why does nothing happen when I press the Windows key and H?**
Make sure the cursor is in a text box. Voice typing won't start anywhere else. Also check that voice access isn't already running.

**Does voice typing work without the internet?**
Standard voice typing needs an internet connection. Voice access works offline once its language files are downloaded.

**Is voice typing free?**
Yes. It's part of Windows 11.

**My tremor affects my voice. Will voice typing still work?**
It may make mistakes more often. Try it with short messages first, and see how well it understands you.

## A note on your health

Voice typing is a way to make the computer easier to use; it doesn't treat a tremor. The NHS notes that a tremor can sometimes affect the voice. If your tremor is new, changing, or affecting your voice or daily life, talk to your doctor.
