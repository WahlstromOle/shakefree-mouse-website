The solution

What if the pointer only followed what you meant?

ShakeFree Mouse sits quietly between your hand and the screen. It filters out the shake and keeps your deliberate movements, so only the movements you mean reach the screen.

It works with the mouse or trackpad you already have. No new hardware.
