A steady cursor, even with shaky hands

ShakeFree Mouse is a small Windows app that smooths out hand tremor before it reaches the mouse pointer, so you can aim, click and drag with confidence.

What it does

  • Smooths shaking. Small tremors are filtered out, while quick, deliberate moves still feel fast. Two simple sliders from 1 to 10 set how much.
  • Clicks land where you aim. Optional “hold to click” keeps the pointer still while you press, and accidental double clicks can be ignored.
  • Turns on and off in a moment. Use the tray icon or the shortcut Ctrl+Shift+F9.
  • Try it right away. A practice area in the app shows your hand and the smoothed pointer side by side.
  • Fits your Windows. Follows light and dark mode, high contrast, larger text and your accent colour.
  • Private. No ads, no tracking. Your settings stay on your PC.
