How it works

Three calm steps. No new hardware.

ShakeFree Mouse runs quietly in the background and works with the mouse or trackpad you already use.

  1. 01

    Get it from Microsoft Store

    Install it in a couple of clicks. Your 5-day free trial starts right away, with everything included and no card needed.

  2. 02

    Set it to your hand

    Two simple sliders from 1 to 10 set how much smoothing you want. A practice area shows your hand and the smoothed pointer side by side.

  3. 03

    Move as you mean to

    Small tremors are filtered out, while quick, deliberate moves still feel fast. Small buttons and links are easier to reach.

  • Smooths shakingFilters out small tremors without slowing your deliberate movements.
  • Hold to clickKeeps the pointer still while you press, so clicks land where you aim.
  • No accidental double clicksA quick, unintended second click can be ignored, so files don’t open by mistake.
  • On and off in a momentUse the tray icon or the shortcut Ctrl+Shift+F9.
  • Fits your WindowsFollows light and dark mode, high contrast, larger text and your accent colour.
  • PrivateNo ads, no tracking. Your settings stay on your PC.
