If ShakeFree Mouse is installed, it opens and activates by itself. If your browser asks “Open ShakeFree Mouse?”, click Open.

Your key

Already have ShakeFree Mouse?

Open ShakeFree Mouse

If nothing happens:

  1. Click Copy key above.
  2. Open ShakeFree Mouse from the tray: the round blue icon by the clock (click the ^ arrow if you can’t see it).
  3. Click Enter key. The key fills in by itself.
  4. Click Activate.

Don’t have it yet?

ShakeFree Mouse is downloaded free from Microsoft Store.

Download from Microsoft Store
  1. Install ShakeFree Mouse from Microsoft Store.
  2. Come back to this page and click Open ShakeFree Mouse, or click Activate in your receipt email again. The app opens and activates by itself.
  3. Or open the app, click Enter key and paste your key.

Your key is also in your receipt email, so you can activate any time later. It works on up to 3 PCs.
