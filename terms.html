Terms & refunds

Last updated: 27 October 2026

These terms explain how buying and using ShakeFree Mouse works. In short: try everything free for 5 days first, because purchases are not refunded. You can cancel a subscription at any time.

Who you’re buying from

ShakeFree Mouse is made by Wahlstrom Digital, Sweden. Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record: it takes your payment, handles taxes and sends your receipt. Lemon Squeezy’s buyer terms also apply to your purchase.

Free trial

ShakeFree Mouse is downloaded free from Microsoft Store. The first time you start it, you get 5 days with every feature included. No card is needed. When the trial ends, ShakeFree Mouse pauses and your mouse works as normal until you enter a key.

Plans and payment

  • Monthly: $4, renewed every month.
  • Yearly: $36, renewed every year.
  • Lifetime: $59, paid once and never renewed.

Every plan includes every feature. Prices are shown at checkout and may include local taxes.

Your key

After buying, you get a key by email. One key works on up to 3 PCs at a time. To move it to another PC, choose Remove from this PC in the app’s settings first. Please don’t share your key beyond your own PCs.

Cancelling

You can cancel a Monthly or Yearly plan at any time, from Manage plan in the app or the link in your receipt email. It then won’t renew, and you keep using ShakeFree Mouse until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Refunds

Because you can try everything free for 5 days before buying, purchases and renewals are not refunded, and unused time on a cancelled plan is not paid back.

If something has gone wrong with a payment, for example you were charged twice or by mistake, contact us and we’ll put it right. This policy doesn’t affect any rights you have under the consumer law where you live.

What ShakeFree Mouse is

ShakeFree Mouse is an assistive app that makes the mouse pointer steadier. It is not a medical device or treatment. We work hard to make it reliable, but we can’t promise it will work with every program, mouse or setup. As far as the law allows, our responsibility is limited to the amount you paid.

Changes

If these terms change, we’ll update them here and change the date at the top. Changes never affect a period you’ve already paid for.
